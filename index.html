SkillForge Social Media Content Plan

A professional 30-day LinkedIn + Instagram content strategy built around trust, engagement, proof, and conversion.

View 30-Day Plan

1. Brand Overview

SkillForge is an EdTech and career-development platform that helps engineering, BCA, and MCA students learn by building real projects with mentorship and verified certification.

TaglineBuild Skills. Forge Careers.
PlatformsLinkedIn + Instagram
ToneEmpowering, relatable, credible

2. Target Audience

LinkedIn: Trust Engine

Audience: TPOs, faculty, HoDs, HRs and education decision-makers.

Need: Risk reduction, proof, legitimacy, and clear outcomes.

Instagram: Engagement Layer

Audience: Students aged 18-25 from Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.

Need: Relatable proof, peer examples, reels, stories, and career motivation.

3. Content Pillars

Problem-First Thought Leadership

Explain why students struggle to apply theory in real projects before presenting SkillForge as the solution.

UGC & Social Proof

Show real student work, rough builds, project screenshots, and intern learning moments.

CTA Psychology

Use low-friction actions like Start, Get, Claim, Save, Comment, and DM instead of hard selling.

8. How to Express the Strategy

Start with the problem, then show proof, then provide a clear action. The best explanation is simple: “Students are not struggling because they lack courses; they struggle because they lack real projects. SkillForge solves this by helping them build proof of skill.”
