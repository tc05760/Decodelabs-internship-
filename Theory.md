# Theory: How to Express the Thoughts

Prepared by Tanya Chauhan

## Core Idea
A social media content plan should not only post random content. It should guide the audience from problem awareness to trust and finally to action.

## Simple Expression Formula
1. Start with the problem: What is the audience struggling with?
2. Add proof: Why should they trust the brand?
3. Give value: What useful idea or tip can they use now?
4. End with a CTA: What small action should they take?

## Example
Students are not struggling because they lack courses. They struggle because they cannot convert theory into real projects. SkillForge solves this by giving guided projects, mentorship, and proof of work.

## Platform Thought Process
LinkedIn is used for trust. It should speak to TPOs, faculty, HRs, and decision-makers. Instagram is used for engagement. It should speak to students using reels, carousels, real project visuals, and relatable captions.

## Why This Project Works
The plan follows four stages: Problem Awareness, Authority Building, Technical Value, and Conversion. This makes the campaign structured and professional.
